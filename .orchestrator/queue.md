  **89a. ⛔ QUESTION FOR THE USER — the row-count tradeoff. The independent re-verify swept 54 dial combinations and disproved that: row count is set by AMPLITUDE alone (`pitch = 2·amp + 0.1·amp`), so three rows at 800px needs `amp ≈ 123` at ANY period, and coverage is then a function of the period. Every three-row candidate that also covers has slope 5.18–6.15, i.e. tall narrow spikes, which is the opposite of "broader and quieter". So the choice is: **(a) SIX broad quiet rows (shipped), (b) THREE spiky rows with equal-or-better coverage, or (c) three rows kept broad and accept bounded blank bands (`170/123` leaves a 119px band).** Purely a look call.
