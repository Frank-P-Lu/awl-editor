- **ITEM 294 — A CRISP PICKER FROSTS ITS OWN FOOTPRINT (`render/blur/extent.rs`).** Frost was a property of the PLATE, so on a composition that draws none the theme and caret pickers — the two CRISP exceptions, which stay unfrosted because their whole job is previewing live world colours — drew their rows straight over live prose and the two interleaved glyph-for-glyph. The frost now has TWO EXTENTS carried as one value, `blur::Frost`: `Full` (the historical whole-canvas takeover — a blur-eligible overlay, the held HUD, the lifetime card, the hold-⌘ peek) and `Footprint`, the DRAWN SURFACES' own box (⚠️ **not `overlay_card_rect`'s** — item 318 widens it where it must to seat the card's upright chrome, and item 343 narrows it to what the frame actually drew; see both bullets below) and nothing beyond its own feathered skirt, so the page around the card keeps the live colours the picker exists to show. **`TextPipeline::frost_mode` is the one owner of whether and where**; it asks every full-takeover condition FIRST (so every frame that frosted before still does, byte-identically) and only then the footprint arm. ⚠️ **The footprint arm is NOT reached only by the crisp pickers.** It is reached by every card that DECLINES the takeover — `overlay_declines_takeover`, one predicate read by both arms so they cannot both fire or both miss: the two CRISP pickers, whose rows preview the live page, and the POINTER-ANCHORED right-click menu, which is not a takeover at all (four rows under the pointer, dismissed by the next click). **Declining the takeover is not declining the frost:** `footprint_frost_applies` then answers `None` on a composition that backs its own rows — its panel or plates already cover the footprint, which is why a right-click menu now leaves the document alone on 17 worlds — and `Footprint` on the three that draw neither, where its rows would otherwise interleave with the document glyph-for-glyph. The sidecar's `dim_overlay` is that same predicate rather than a second copy of it. **ENROLMENT IS ASKED OF THE ROSTER, never of a named world:** `blur::footprint_frost_applies` is true exactly when the composition puts nothing opaque between its rows and the document — `list_backing() != Card` AND `!draws_row_plates()` — which is `Diagonal` (Mangrove, Magpie) and `Ruled` (Paperbark) today. A `Pane` world is excluded by its panel and a `Bars` world by its plates; both are byte-identical (measured: 0 of 960 000 pixels differ on eight worlds' pickers, and on the full palette). The 1-bit exclusion sits ABOVE both arms (a gaussian of pure black/white smears into forbidden grey at any extent) and is law-tested by FORCING a bare list style onto Wagtail, since its own `Pane` composition makes that ordering otherwise unreachable. **Originally scoped by SCISSOR ALONE, which item 312 replaced with a feathered mask** — the reasoning recorded here ("a rect uniform would produce the same hard edge anyway") is true of a rect uniform and false of a feathered one; the scissor survives as a conservative bound. ⚠️ **The scissor is PASS state, not draw state** — the card, the canvas-anchored wordmark and the whole chrome tail share that pass, so it is RESET to the target before returning; deleting the reset left all 1007 render tests green (the card's rows are inside the box already) and clipped the toast off the frame, which is now its own law. `FOOTPRINT_DIM` is ZERO where the full takeover recedes by `DIM`: a footprint is a hole in a page whose live colours are still the subject a hair outside its edge, so it is a pure defocus, and the frosted patch's mean colour in LINEAR light equals the live page's to within Δa\*/Δb\* 0.5.

- **ITEM 294's other half — THE FROST'S REACH WAS A DEVICE-PIXEL LENGTH.** The Gaussian's reach is a fixed count of quarter-res texels, so a fixed `DOWNSAMPLE` made it constant in PHYSICAL px and therefore HALVED the defocus a reader perceives on a 2x display — invisible to every capture, all of which run at `--capture-dpi 1`. `blur::extent::downsample_for(dpi)` multiplies the authored logical reach by DPI exactly once, and the recreate key is now `(size, downsample)` because a display move between a 1x and a 2x screen changes the working size without changing the surface's. `dpi == 1` returns the authored constant untouched, so every capture and every 1x frame is byte-identical; a 2x FULL-takeover frost genuinely changes (measured: 224 249 of 960 000 pixels on Gumtree's palette at `--capture-dpi 2`), in the direction of the frost the same overlay already shows at 1x.
